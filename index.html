About Me

Im Neomii, I am a senior Marketing student at DePaul
University graduating with a Bachelors in Digital Marketing. I
have a strong interest in brand strategy, consumer behavior,
and research marketing.Ive been able to explore these
interests both in the classroom and outside.

I have built hands on experience throughout my career doing
content creation for Oud Cafe, and BobaNova. In these roles I
have supported brand visibility, developed social content, and
learned how to think strategically about how businesses show
up for their audiences.

Some of
my work

A typical day in my internship
usually starts with looking at
what is happening in the cafe
that could be turned into
content. Since the space is
always active, there are a lot of
natural moments to capture.

From there, I film short clips,
think through captions, and
create posts or reels that match
Oud’s brand voice. Some of my
work is planned ahead, but a lot
of it also depends on noticing
small moments that could
connect with people online.

My internship is a mix of in-
person and online work. The in-
person side is important
because I need to actually be in
the cafe to capture real content
and understand the customer
experience. The online side
comes in when I am editing
videos, writing captions,
checking engagement,
brainstorming content ideas,
and thinking about what types
of posts perform best.

Oud is a smaller business
compared to a large corporate
company. Because the company
is more local, I get to see how
marketing decisions can directly
impact the business’s image and
customer connection.
