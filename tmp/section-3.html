SOME of
my work

A typical day in my internship
usually starts with looking at
what is happening in the cafe
that could be turned into
content. Since the space is
always active, there are a lot of
natural moments to capture.


From there, I film short clips,
think through captions, and
create posts or reels that match
Oud’s brand voice. Some of my
work is planned ahead, but a lot
of it also depends on noticing
small moments that could
connect with people online.
