usually starts with looking at
what is happening in the cafe
that could be turned into

From there, I film short clips,

My internship is a mix of in-person and online work. The in-person side is important because I need to actually be in the cafe to capture real content and understand the customer experience. The online side comes in when I am editing videos, writing captions, checking engagement, brainstorming content ideas, and thinking about what types of posts perform best. Oud is a smaller business compared to a large corporate company. Because the company is more local, I get to see how marketing decisions can directly impact the business’s image and customer connection.
